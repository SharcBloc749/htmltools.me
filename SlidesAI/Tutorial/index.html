How to use SlidesAI

Generate a fully-themed slide deck from a single line of text, then browse or reuse anything the community has made in the Library.

Getting started
1
Describe your topic ▼

Go to Generate and type a short description of what your deck is about — a topic, a vibe, or both. The more specific, the better the result: try deep sea fishing gear for beginners rather than just fishing.

Screenshot coming soon
2
Click Generate ▼

SlidesAI designs a full theme — color palette, font pairing, icon style — and writes 4 slides of real, specific content: a title slide plus three more with varied layouts (stat, quote, list, or two-column).

Each themed slide also gets either a hand-composed illustration or a set of matching icons, generated to fit that exact slide's content.

Screenshot coming soon
3
Review and download ▼

Click through the thumbnails to preview each slide. When you're happy, hit Download HTML to save a self-contained deck file you can open in any browser or share directly.

Not quite right? Click Regenerate to get a fresh take on the same topic.

The Library
4
Every deck is public ▼

Every deck generated on SlidesAI — by anyone — is automatically added to the Library so the whole community can browse and reuse it. There's no way to opt out, and no one can see who made a given template.

Note: Don't put sensitive or personal information in your prompt or slide content, since the resulting deck will be visible to everyone.
Screenshot coming soon
5
Search by theme ▼

Search matches against the deck's name and its visual keywords — so searching sea surfaces anything themed around water, fishing, coral, or the ocean, even if the word "sea" never appears in the title.

6
View and download ▼

Click any template in the Library to open a full preview. From there you can page through its slides and download it as a standalone HTML file, just like your own decks.
