Shortcuts — type the word at the end of your search

Capitalisation doesn't matter. Cats MatTube works.
Only the very end counts. Any character after the word, like banana image? or bing., turns the shortcut off and brings you here instead.
Just the word (e.g. translate) opens that tool's front page. Every web engine word works no matter which one you picked as default.
