Shortcuts — trailing keywords and !bangs

Destination keywords: put one at the end, like cute cats mattube; capitalization does not matter.
!bangs: put one at the start, like !yt cute cats or !wiki Halifax. They don't hijack ordinary searches.
Just the keyword opens the destination's home page. Any character after a trailing keyword disables that shortcut.
